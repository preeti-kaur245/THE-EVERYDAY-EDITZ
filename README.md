# Everyday Editz 🛍️
### Everyday Essentials. Smarter Shopping.

Everyday Editz is a modern, customer-focused e-commerce platform designed to make everyday shopping simple, convenient and accessible. The platform brings together carefully selected products across Women, Men, Kids, and Home & Lifestyle categories through a clean, responsive shopping experience.

Our goal is to create a convenient online shopping destination where customers can discover useful products, explore affordable everyday essentials and enjoy a seamless shopping journey.

---

## 🌐 Project Overview

**Everyday Editz** is an e-commerce website built with a focus on simplicity, accessibility, responsive design and customer convenience.

The platform aims to combine the familiar shopping experience of major e-commerce marketplaces with a clean, minimal and easy-to-use interface.

### ✨ Key Features

- **Modern Homepage:** Clean storefront with product highlights and featured collections.
- **Product Categories:** Dedicated shopping sections for Women, Men, Kids, and Home & Lifestyle.
- **Product Listings:** Browse products with images, descriptions and pricing.
- **Shopping Cart:** Add, remove and manage selected products.
- **Wishlist:** Save favourite products for later.
- **Customer Authentication:** Simple email and password-based sign-in and account creation.
- **Guest Browsing:** Explore the store without creating an account.
- **Staff Portal:** Separate administrative access for managing store operations.
- **Responsive Interface:** Optimized layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop.
- **Product Management:** Administrative product management capabilities.
- **Background Audio:** Automatic playback where supported by browser permissions.
- **Smart Navigation:** Improved navigation and back-button behaviour.

## 📱 User Experience

The platform is designed around three main experiences:

**1. Customer Experience**
- Browse products.
- Explore categories.
- Manage cart and wishlist.
- Create an account or sign in.
- Navigate through the storefront.

**2. Guest Experience**
- Explore products without registration.
- Discover categories.
- Add products to the shopping experience where supported.

**3. Staff/Admin Experience**
- Access a protected staff portal.
- Manage product information.
- Maintain storefront content.
- Perform authorized administrative operations.
